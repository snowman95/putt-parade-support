About this app

Putt Parade is a free game by megahit. The game does not require an account and has no game server. Hole results, stars, ghost shots, unlocked balls and settings are stored on your device. The game does not send these records anywhere. Your device backup service may keep a copy.

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or notifications.

Advertising and consent

The app offers optional rewarded ads, to take back your last shot. It uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) to show them and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage consent where required. Google and its partners may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics to serve, measure and protect ads. Ad availability depends on consent, region and network access. You can keep playing without watching an ad.

Where the app offers privacy choices, a Privacy Options button appears on the main menu. You can also manage advertising and tracking choices in your device settings. See how Google uses data from partner apps.

Children

Putt Parade is not directed to children under 13, and the game itself does not collect personal information from anyone.

Requests and updates

For questions or requests about this app's data, contact snowman95tube@gmail.com. Local progress can be removed by deleting the app, subject to your device backup settings. We will post policy changes on this page with an updated effective date.
